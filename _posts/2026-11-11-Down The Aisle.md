> "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life." 
- John 3:16 *(NIV)*

I am walking down the aisle tomorrow and as I'm finally processing how close it is to the day I get married all I long to do is write to you Jesus. To process these last 42 minutes of singleness before my life changes and I become one with him. I'm filled with joy and excitement but also fear. Fear of all the what ifs or what nots. The way my brain processes things to prepare for the worst in a time like this is hard to maneuver around.The if it didn't work for my parents why would it work for me? But as the thoughts come creeping in trying to take me into the never-ending unknown. I'm remined of you Jesus and how yes, this life is hard, broken and sinful. The way I love and will be loved is flawed because we are flawed humans just amplifies YOUR perfect unfailing love. Are need for you in our lives but also our marriage. A love so deep so perfect so unconditional it doesn't make sense. My brain will never fully comprehend how intense it is how big, how strong, how wide, or how vast. I was told once before that “Our God is so strong it'll take us a lifetime to understand just a little.” As I prepare for tomorrow, I'm in Awe of you God and your people the way they love and allow themselves to be used by you. Your love for Justin and I shown through them coming together and helping this wedding be put together so perfectly I'm AWE of you God. You provided us with a community that helped pull off a wedding in less than a week's notice. That was my prayer that you Lord would plan my wedding because I knew then that nothing is impossible for you and you did it all and provided for it all. Thank you Jesus. I still don't get it. Why me? That you sent your one and only son to die for me. You blessed me with a life you've redeemed. Now all I ask is that you use it every part even the most painful things for your Glory God. That is what I long for to not only be loved by you but to be used by you.

> "So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God."
- 1 Corinthians 10:31 *(NIV)*

We got married June 28th 2026 everything came together so perfectly. Only God could've planned it out so beautifully.
