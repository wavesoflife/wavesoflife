This post is your formatting reference guide. Every time you want to do something with the *look* of your writing — bold a word, add a list, include a link — come back here.

You don't need to memorize any of this. Just bookmark (or keep) this post and look things up as you need them.

## How Markdown Works

When you write a post, you're writing in something called **Markdown**. Markdown is just regular text with a few simple symbols sprinkled in. The site automatically converts those symbols into beautiful formatting.

For example, you type:

```
I **love** this verse.
```

And on the page it looks like:

I **love** this verse.

That's all it is! No special software. No buttons. Just symbols.

---

## Headings

Headings help organize your post into sections. Put a `#` symbol (and a space) before any line to make it a heading.

| What you type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| `## A Big Section` | A large bold heading |
| `### A Smaller Section` | A medium bold heading |
| `#### Even Smaller` | A smaller heading |

**Tip:** Use `##` for your main sections and `###` for sub-points within a section. You generally won't need anything smaller than `###` in a blog post.

## Bold and Italic

**Bold** draws attention to important words. *Italic* is for gentle emphasis, titles of books, or journal-style thoughts.

| What you type | What you see |
|---|---|
| `**important word**` | **important word** |
| `*gentle emphasis*` | *gentle emphasis* |
| `***bold and italic***` | ***bold and italic*** |

**Example in a sentence:**

You type:
```
God's love is **unconditional** — not earned, not lost.
It is *always* reaching toward us.
```

It looks like:

God's love is **unconditional** — not earned, not lost.
It is *always* reaching toward us.

## Bullet Lists

Use a `-` (hyphen) at the start of each item. Leave a blank line before the list.

```
Here are three things I'm grateful for today:

- The quiet of early morning prayer
- A kind word from a friend
- Unexpected grace in a hard moment
```

Looks like:

Here are three things I'm grateful for today:

- The quiet of early morning prayer
- A kind word from a friend
- Unexpected grace in a hard moment

### Numbered Lists

Use `1.`, `2.`, `3.` instead:

```
1. Open your Bible
2. Read slowly
3. Write down what stands out
```

1. Open your Bible
2. Read slowly
3. Write down what stands out

## Bible Verse Blockquotes

This is one of the loveliest features of the site. Start a line with `>` and a space to create a verse quote with the beautiful teal border styling.

```
> "The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing."

- Psalm 23:1 *(NIV)*
```

Looks like:

> "The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing."

- Psalm 23:1 *(NIV)*

**Tips for Bible verses:**
- Put the `>` before each line of the verse if it spans multiple lines.
- Add the reference on its own line below (starting with `-`) so it's visually separated.
- You can note the translation in parentheses — ESV, NIV, NLT, KJV, etc.

Here's a multi-line example:

```
> "Even though I walk through the darkest valley,
> I will fear no evil, for you are with me;
> your rod and your staff, they comfort me."

- Psalm 23:4 *(NIV)*
```

> "Even though I walk through the darkest valley,
> I will fear no evil, for you are with me;
> your rod and your staff, they comfort me."

- Psalm 23:4 *(NIV)*

## Links

To link to a website, use this format:

```
[the words that are clickable](https://the-web-address.com)
```

**Examples:**

```
Read this passage on [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com).
```

Read this passage on [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com).

```
[Email me anytime](mailto:zayra@wavesoflife.blog) — I'd love to hear from you.
```

[Email me anytime](mailto:zayra@wavesoflife.blog) — I'd love to hear from you.

To link to another page on *your own site*, use the page path:

```
You can learn more on my [About page](/about/).
```

## Horizontal Rules (Dividers)

To add a visual dividing line between sections, type three dashes on their own line:

```
---
```

This blog post uses them to separate each major section. They help readers know when one topic ends and another begins.

## A Note About Line Breaks

In Markdown, pressing **Enter once** does *not* create a new paragraph. You need a **blank line** between paragraphs.

This won't work as two paragraphs:
```
Line one.
Line two.
```

This will:
```
Line one.

Line two.
```

When in doubt, add a blank line — it never hurts!
